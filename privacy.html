Privacy

Relevé Scribe for Android, and this website. Last updated November 20, 2026.

The short version

Scribe does not collect your data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server of ours that your notes or recordings are sent to. Your voice is turned into text on your phone, and the text is saved in a folder you choose.

What Scribe does with your voice

  • Scribe uses the microphone only while you are recording.
  • Speech is converted to text on your device. The audio is not uploaded.
  • While you record, the audio is held in a temporary file on your phone so it can be turned into text. It is deleted once the note is written.
  • If a note could not be written (a save that failed, a recording you cancelled, or one where nothing was heard), the audio file stays on your phone for up to a day and is deleted the next time you open Scribe after that. It is not uploaded.
  • Scribe keeps the text of your notes, not a recording of your voice.

Where your notes are kept

  • Each notebook is a plain text file in the folder you pick during setup.
  • Scribe also keeps a small history on your phone so it can show recent notes. It stays on the device and is removed when you uninstall the app.
  • Your word list (the dictionary) is stored on your phone.
  • If the folder you pick is synced by another service, such as Google Drive, that service handles the files under its own privacy terms. Scribe itself does not sync or upload anything.

When Scribe uses the internet

Only to download a speech model, which is a file the app needs in order to understand speech. This happens when you set the app up or choose a different model. The file is downloaded from Hugging Face, a public host for such models. As with any download, that host sees your device's internet address. Nothing about you or your notes is sent. After the download, Scribe works without a connection.

Permissions

  • Microphone: to record your voice when you ask it to.
  • Notifications: to show that a recording is in progress.
  • Internet: to download a speech model, as described above.
  • The folder you choose: to read and write your notebook files there, and nowhere else.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • No automatic crash reports sent to us.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because we do not have any.

If you write to us

If you send feedback by email, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to reply and to improve the app.

Beta testing

If you join the test through Google Play, Google handles your enrollment and may share with us the feedback you submit there, under Google's own privacy policy.

This website

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking scripts. Its fonts are served from the site itself. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log visitors' internet addresses to operate and protect the service.

Children

Scribe is a general note-taking tool and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If the app changes in a way that affects any of this, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
