Relevé, ballet: to rise

A notebook from twenty years ago still opens.

Most of what we write now lives inside apps and accounts, and lasts as long as they do. It no longer has to.

The tools that listen, sort and help you think can now run on the device in your hand, and save what you make as plain files you can read, keep and carry anywhere.

The idea we are testing

A tool can be fully capable and still leave everything with you.

Relevé is the ballet step where you rise under your own strength. The barre steadies you; it does not lift you. We think a tool should work the same way.

Yours

Your work lives as files on your device, in a folder you pick. Nothing to sign up for.

Readable

Plain text that people and AI can both read. Open it with anything, take it anywhere.

Human first

The tool handles the busywork. The thinking stays with you.
